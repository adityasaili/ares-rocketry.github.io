Project Odyssey

Meet Genesis — ARES's first entirely student-built hybrid rocket engine. No kit, no shortcuts: designed, machined and tested by the students flying it. Full-scale hot fire testing is underway right now.

Odyssey pairs Genesis with a new side-hatch parachute deployment system, retractable air brakes, and a custom telemetry network built entirely in-house.

Target: 10,000 ft hybrid category, AURC 2026, White Cliffs NSW
Led by: Propulsion — Brooke Doolan & Steven Wang
